# DevDeck 1.37.8

- After a reboot, sign-in or quit, the cockpit reopens the sessions that were open, as long as their conversation was used in the last two days. Pinned sessions always come back. Before, nothing came back until Restore all was pressed.
- "Used" is read from the conversation's own transcript, not from terminal output. A terminal redraws on its own, so a session left untouched for days still looked active.
- Sessions left out stay in Previous sessions with a notice; one click opens any of them.
- A machine reopens its own sessions when it launches, so a paired deck watching that machine gets them back without pressing Restore all over the link.
- Restore all and the restart after an update are explicit requests and still reopen everything they name.

Every reopened session is a whole agent process (about 1 GB for Claude, plus each configured MCP server). Restore all reopens every saved session, including ones idle for days.
