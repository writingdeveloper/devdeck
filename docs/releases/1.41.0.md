# DevDeck 1.41.0

## Quick replies

- **One click for what you keep typing.** A 💬 button in a session's header opens your phrases: "Yes, go ahead", "Commit it", "Run the tests and check the result", "Summarize what you changed". Picking one types it and presses Enter. A green dot on the button means the session is waiting for you.
- **Ctrl+Shift+1…9** in the terminal sends phrase 1 to 9 without opening the menu. The digit is never typed into the agent.
- **Your own phrases.** Settings → Quick replies takes one phrase per line, up to nine. Leave it empty for the defaults in your language.

## Session timeline

- A panel over the right edge of the terminal lists every prompt you sent in the session. Each entry shows when you sent it, its first line, the agent's last words in reply, and how many images that turn produced.
- Click a prompt to scroll the terminal to it, briefly highlighted. Click the image count to open that turn's screenshot.
- The list comes from the session's own record, so it is complete even when the terminal's scrollback no longer reaches back. Prompts that have scrolled out are shown dimmed and cannot be jumped to.
- The panel floats over the terminal and never resizes it.

## Mark up a screenshot and send it back

- In the full-size viewer, **Mark up** gives you a box, an arrow and a pen in red. Undo is Ctrl+Z, and Esc stops drawing.
- **Attach to the session** sends the image, with your marks, to the session it came from. The image is written where the session runs and its path is typed into the prompt, the same way a pasted screenshot is. This works for sessions on another PC too.
- The marks are drawn in the image's own pixels and sent at full resolution, then scaled down only if the picture would exceed the 5MB attachment limit.
- **Copy image** copies the marked-up version when there are marks.

## Documents for a session on another PC

- 📎 and drag-and-drop for a session on another PC now take text, log, data and PDF files as well as images: `.txt .log .md .json .jsonl .csv .tsv .yaml .xml .toml .ini .cfg .conf .diff .patch .sql .pdf`, up to 5MB each.
- The other PC writes the file into its temp folder under its own name and types that path. Old files are cleaned up after a day, like pasted images.
- That PC checks every file before writing it:
  - only the kinds listed above are accepted, never scripts, executables or HTML
  - a PDF must start like a PDF, and a text file must contain no binary bytes
  - it needs the same "type in sessions" permission as an image, and shares the same hourly limit
- A PC still on an older DevDeck cannot receive documents. You are told how many files it could not take and that the PC needs updating; nothing is typed into the session for them.

## Under the hood

- A new automated check (`qa:cockpit-tools`) runs on every change on Windows. A fixture agent records every byte typed into it, and the check covers:
  - both ways of sending a quick reply, and phrases edited in Settings
  - the timeline's contents, and that clicking a prompt jumps to it
  - that a marked-up screenshot reaches the session with its red marks in the pixels
- The remote-image check (`qa:images-remote`) now also sends a log file from one PC to another over the real link.
- Turns are read in the same incremental pass over the session record as the images. The timeline costs no second read of a transcript that can reach hundreds of megabytes. The performance gate is unchanged.
