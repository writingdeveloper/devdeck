# DevDeck 1.49.0

## Close several sessions at once

The sidebar could already close a hand-picked set of sessions — after a Ctrl+click that nothing on screen mentioned. So most decks were closed one row at a time, and a deck of twenty sessions stayed a deck of twenty sessions.

- **A Select button above the session groups** turns on a selection mode. Every row shows a checkbox, a plain click picks a row instead of opening it, the box on a group heading picks the whole group (opening it in full first, so nothing is picked that cannot be seen), Shift+click picks a range, and **Select all** opens every group and picks everything. **Close** closes the picked rows after one question; **Done** or Esc leaves the mode; Delete closes the picked rows from the keyboard. Every row's ⋯ menu also has **Select…**, which picks that row and starts the mode. Ctrl+click and Shift+click work as before, in and out of the mode.
- **Local, remote, background and saved rows mix freely**, and the one question says what the set holds — *2 on this PC · 3 on SIHYEONG-4080 · 1 background · 4 saved* — because a close on another PC stops an agent in front of someone else. The tiles go in one batch and the pane settles once at the end (it used to re-select and re-render after each one, a visible stall for a dozen). Closes on other PCs answer later; a group of refusals or owed closes is one line, not a stack. Background sessions close with one progress line and one summary, machines side by side and one after another within a machine, so the first refusal starts Claude Code's background service once and the rest find it running.
- **A tile attached to a Claude background session** (one opened from the Background group) used to leave the session running when closed; its row came straight back under Background, to be closed a second time. The question now offers to end the session as well, ticked by default. The conversation is kept either way.
- Closing one picked row asks that row's own question (*Close session 'x'?*) rather than *Close 1 selected sessions?*.

## The Background listing no longer starts a process on every poll

`claude agents --json --all` is a Claude Code process — about 340 ms of a 300 MB executable — that every poll started, on this PC and on every paired one, nearly always to answer what it had answered the time before. DevDeck now looks first at what that command reads: the job records under `~/.claude/jobs`, one folder per background session with its `state.json` (a readdir and a stat per record, about a millisecond). No records means no sessions and no process at all; records unchanged since the last listing means the last answer, for up to five minutes; only a change runs the command. On a PC with no background sessions the process never starts.

## From a review of the sidebar and the close flows

- **The confirmation could be answered by an Enter that was not aimed at it.** Ctrl+Shift+P pressed while a question was open moved focus to the quick-open field behind it, and the Enter meant for that field closed the sessions. Chords no longer leave the question, an Enter from outside it only brings focus back to it, a held key's auto-repeat is ignored, and a second question is never stacked on the first — a row's ⋯ menu stayed open above the dialog and could ask one; it closes first now, and the dialog sits above every menu. After Cancel the keyboard goes back to where it was, so the next Delete or Enter acts on the same thing.
- **A row's ⋯ menu was clipped when the list was short.** The session list is its own scrolling box, sized to its content; a menu dropped from the last row ran past its bottom and one flipped up from the first row ran past its top. On a fresh install with one session only the bottom item (Close) showed. The menu is placed against the window now, below the ⋯ when there is room and above it otherwise, and closes when the list scrolls.
- **Keyboard focus survived a re-render only on a row.** Every publish that reorders rows (every 30 s with two active sessions) blurred the ⋯ button, an open menu's item or a group heading's controls; a keyboard user lost the menu they were in. They are put back now.
- **Forgetting a saved entry from its ⋯ menu** — the one destructive row action with no question, right under Pin — offers Undo.
- **Bulk flows toasted once per row**: restoring saved entries for a PC that is offline, or closing a group on a PC that refuses, stacked a notice per session past the top of the window. They show one line for the set; the diagnostics log keeps the rest.
- **A pinned row wears its pin.** The groups that come before Pinned (needs you, working, another PC) keep their rows, so pinning one moved nothing and read as having done nothing.
- Smaller: the Korean group confirmations use the same register as every other question; a group section is named for screen readers by its name and count alone, not by its buttons as well; rows carry a pressed state only while picking; the selection bar's Close is drawn as the destructive action it is.
