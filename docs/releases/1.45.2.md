# DevDeck 1.45.2

## An update no longer doubles the sessions running on a paired PC

After **Update all** (or any update relaunch), every session on the other PC came back twice: the one that was already running there, and a new, empty one under the same name. Each update doubled them — measured 7 → 14 → 29 sessions running on one machine across two updates, every extra one a whole Claude process.

- **Cause:** two things reopened the same saved tile at once. When this PC relaunched after the update, the other PC — which had kept running — announced its sessions, and DevDeck adopted each one as a tile. At the same time the update relaunch walked its list of saved tiles and restored them one by one, including the ones on the other PC. By the time a remote tile's turn came, its conversation was already a tile here (the adopted one), and the restore read that as "this conversation is open in another tile, so start a fresh one" — a new conversation on the other PC, carrying the same name and label.
- **Fix:** an update relaunch restores only this PC's own tiles. A tile on another PC is left as a saved entry and comes back by adoption the moment that PC announces it, keeping its label and pin. If that PC is updating too, it restores its own tiles on its launch and announces them — same result, no duplicates.
- **Also:** any restore — a click on a previous session, Restore all, the launch restore — now stops when the conversation is already a live tile here, instead of starting a second one. The log says so: `already open here as <id>; nothing started`.

## Cleaning up what earlier versions left behind

The duplicates created before this fix are still running on the other PC. They are the empty sessions with no history. In the cockpit sidebar, Ctrl/Shift-click them and close them together. Each closed one frees a Claude process on that PC.
